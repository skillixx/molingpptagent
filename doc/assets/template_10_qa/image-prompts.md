# 清新校园教育模板图片提示词

## 共同约束

- 使用内置图片生成工具；工具未暴露模型标识时记录“工具未暴露”。
- 清新现代教育插画，白色、草木绿、嫩绿、湖水蓝和少量暖黄色。
- 不含文字、字母、数字、Logo、水印、校徽、按钮、真实人物、假截图和假数据。
- 不复制参考稿卡通角色或其他版权角色。
- 背景保留连续文字安全区；透明装饰必须是真实透明背景并具有干净 Alpha 边缘。

## 1. 封面背景

目标文件：`template_10_asset_bg_cover_v1.jpg`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 16:9 PowerPoint cover background
Primary request: an original clean modern education-themed background with a white paper-like base, a restrained fresh green tree canopy along the very top edge, and two or three soft rolling green hills along the bottom edge
Style/medium: polished flat hand-drawn editorial illustration, contemporary educational design, not childish clip art
Composition/framing: wide landscape, large uninterrupted low-detail safe area across the center-left for a two-line title, decorative elements confined to the top 15 percent and bottom 18 percent
Color palette: white, grass green, light green, subtle lake blue, tiny warm yellow accents
Constraints: no people, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no school emblem, no photo, no rectangular frame, no busy center
```
